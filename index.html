Freight pricing is a live auction.

Brokers must quote while capacity, fuel, and market rates shift underneath them—often without time to research. That’s why pricing becomes gut feel, and margin leaks by default.

Systematic margin leakage

Even a $30–$50 misprice per load compounds across thousands of shipments.

Quote latency

Manual lane research takes 10–15 minutes; the load is gone.

Agent variance

Two agents quote two prices for the same lane.

Winner’s curse

Win the load—then realize the rate was below cost.

Mid-market freight brokers and 3PLs running high-volume spot quotes where speed + consistency drive profit.
